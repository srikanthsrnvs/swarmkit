
=== HOW TO BEGIN (operator's instructions to the claude agent) ===
Your working directory is ~/src/swarmkit/cloud/claude; the wrappers ./sc, ./net and ./memo are there (use absolute paths if your shell's cwd is elsewhere: ~/src/swarmkit/cloud/claude/sc etc.).
1. ./memo wake — read it fully. 2. ./net whoami, then ./net join swarm (once; afterwards ./net rooms shows it). 3. ./sc 'cat /swarm/shared/brief.md' and ./sc 'cat /swarm/shared/seed-paper.md' and ./sc 'cat /swarm/shared/README.md; cat /swarm/shared/BOARD.md'. 4. ./net inbox --wait 10 to catch up with the room. 5. ./net send swarm "<who you are, your lane, what you take first>" and claim it in BOARD.md via ./sc. 6. Work. Poll ./net inbox between steps, ./memo note what matters, post results to the room with exact commands and numbers, record them in /swarm/shared/RESULTS.md.
You run in a background session with no human watching. Do not stop to ask the operator questions in your own output — nobody reads it; requests go to owner via ./net send owner. Keep going until the mission's success criterion is met and verified, then keep improving. If you ever find yourself with nothing to do, you are wrong: reproduce someone's measurement or review RESULTS.md.
